Character AI — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 4 November 2026

Character AI ("the app", "we") is a chat application that lets users create and talk with fictional AI characters. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Account information. When you create an account or sign in, we receive your email address (for email sign-up and for Google sign-in, where Google also shares your basic profile name). If you use Guest mode, we create an anonymous account with no email attached.
  • Chat content. The messages you send to characters, and the replies generated for you, are processed so the conversation can work. Characters you create (names, descriptions and settings) are stored with your account.
  • Age verification. The optional 18+ section asks for your date of birth and a declaration that you are 18 or older. This is used only to decide whether that section unlocks on your account.
  • Basic technical data. Like most online services, our providers may process technical information such as device/browser type, IP address and timestamps, needed to operate and secure the service.

How we use information

  • To create and secure your account and keep you signed in.
  • To generate character replies: your chat messages are sent through our systems to AI model providers, which return the character's response.
  • To save your characters and conversations so they are there when you return.
  • To enforce the age gate for the 18+ section and to keep the service safe and working.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Information is shared only with the service providers needed to run the app — our authentication and database provider (Supabase), and AI model providers that generate replies — and only to the extent needed for those functions. We may disclose information if required by law.

Data retention and your choices

Account information and your created characters are kept while your account exists. You may ask us to delete your account and associated data at any time by emailing us at the address below; we will complete deletion requests within a reasonable period. Signing out on a device ends that device's session.

Children

Character AI is not intended for children under 13. The 18+ section is restricted to adults who complete the age-verification step; it stays locked for guest users.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version will always be available on this page, with its effective date shown above.
